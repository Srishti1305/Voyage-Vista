Welcome to Varanasi

Varanasi, one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, is a sacred place for Hindus. The ghats along the Ganges River, the ancient temples, and the spiritual ambiance make it a unique destination. Visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple, the Dasaswamedh Ghat, and enjoy the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti.

10 Must-Visit Places in Varanasi

  1. Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  2. Dasaswamedh Ghat
  3. Manikarnika Ghat
  4. Assi Ghat
  5. Ramnagar Fort
  6. Dhamek Stupa
  7. Sarnath
  8. Banaras Hindu University
  9. Tulsi Manas Temple
  10. Alamgir Mosque
