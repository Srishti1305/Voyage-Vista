Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur, known as the "Pink City," is the capital of Rajasthan and a major tourist destination in India. Its palaces, forts, and vibrant bazaars reflect the rich cultural heritage of the Rajputs. Don't miss the iconic Hawa Mahal, the grand Amer Fort, and the stunning City Palace.

10 Must-Visit Places in Jaipur

  2. Amer Fort
  3. City Palace
  4. Jantar Mantar
  5. Jal Mahal
  6. Albert Hall Museum
  7. Birla Mandir
  8. Jaigarh Fort
  9. Nahargarh Fort
  10. Chokhi Dhani
