Welcome to Allahabad

Allahabad, also known as Prayagraj, is a city of great religious and historical significance. It is famous for the Triveni Sangam, the confluence of three sacred rivers. The city also hosts the Kumbh Mela, the largest religious gathering in the world. Must-visit places include the Allahabad Fort, Anand Bhavan, and the Akshaya Vat.

10 Must-Visit Places in Allahabad

  1. Triveni Sangam
  2. Allahabad Fort
  3. Anand Bhavan

  5. Khusro Bagh
