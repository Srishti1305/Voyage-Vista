Welcome to Pune

Pune, known as the "Oxford of the East," is a vibrant city that combines history and modernity. The city is home to prestigious educational institutions, beautiful gardens, and historic forts. Must-visit places include the Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace, and the serene Saras Baug.

10 Must-Visit Places in Pune

  1. Shaniwar Wada
  2. Aga Khan Palace
  3. Saras Baug
  4. Dagadusheth Halwai Ganapati Temple
  5. Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum
  6. Pataleshwar Cave Temple
  7. Osho Ashram
  8. Parvati Hill
  9. Vetal Tekdi
  10. Singhgad Fort
