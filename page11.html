Welcome to Mysore

Mysore, known as the "City of Palaces," is a city steeped in history and culture. The grand Mysore Palace, beautiful gardens, and vibrant markets make it a popular tourist destination. Don't miss the Chamundi Hill, Brindavan Gardens, and the Mysore Zoo.

10 Must-Visit Places in Mysore

  1. Mysore Palace
  2. Chamundi Hill
  3. Brindavan Gardens
  4. Mysore Zoo
  5. St. Philomena's Church
  6. Karanji Lake
  7. Lalitha Mahal
  8. Jaganmohan Palace
  9. Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary
  10. Railway Museum
