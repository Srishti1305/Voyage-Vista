Welcome to Hyderabad

Hyderabad, the "City of Pearls," is known for its rich history, culture, and cuisine. Famous landmarks include the Charminar, the stunning Golconda Fort, and the opulent Chowmahalla Palace. Hyderabad is also renowned for its biryani, a must-try dish for every visitor.

10 Must-Visit Places in Hyderabad

  1. Charminar
  2. Golconda Fort
  3. Chowmahalla Palace
  4. Hussain Sagar Lake
  5. Ramoji Film City
  6. Salar Jung Museum
  7. Birla Mandir
  8. Nehru Zoological Park
  9. Lumbini Park
  10. Shilparamam
